## About me

### Personal

I am from [Ragusa, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ragusa,_Sicily), but I am currently living in [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin).

While I was studying Architecture at school, I began experimenting software alternatives and knowing deeper how computers work. At the end of the school, I decided to leave the career in Architecture and focus my studies mainly on Computer Science and Programming.

Other passions I have are reading, music, and movies.

### Professional

I studied Computer Engineering at [Polytechnique of Turin](https://www.polito.it), with a Master's Degree in Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics.

My thesis for the Master's Degree was inspired by my activity in [Trigenia](https://www.trigenia.it), an Energy Service Company located in Turin. I designed and implemented an infrastructure to collect data from the industrial plants of customers, and then use these data for both real-time processing and offline tasks, like data analytics and training of Machine Learning models. During this project I had the opportunity to build a system based on micro-services and some APIs to allow existing projects to not be completely redesigned in order to work with my infrastructure. I also had the opportunity to study some interesting details about relational databases and data platforms like data warehouses, data lakes and data lakehouses.
